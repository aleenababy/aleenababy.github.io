About Me

I started my journey exploring the largest structures in the universe. With a PhD in computational astrophysics, I built simulations to understand the formation of galaxies — combining physics, mathematics, and code to answer cosmic questions.

Today, I use those same skills to solve industrial problems that are no less complex. As a data scientist and ML engineer in Germany's manufacturing sector, I've developed AI models to predict microporosity in casting, built digital twins to optimize production workflows, and delivered tools that integrate simulation, domain knowledge, and machine learning — in environments where downtime costs millions.

I combine deep technical skills, interdisciplinary thinking, and teaching experience to bridge academia and industry. Whether I'm leading ML projects, teaching Python, or mentoring PhDs transitioning to industry, I bring clarity, energy, and impact. If you're looking for someone who can connect science, software, and storytelling — we should talk.
